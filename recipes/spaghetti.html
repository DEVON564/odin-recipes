Classic Spaghetti Anglio e Olio

A simple yet flavorful Italian pasta dish made with spaghetti, garlic, olive oil, and a touch of chili flakes, delivering a light and aromatic taste in every bite.

Ingridients

  • 400 g (14 oz) spaghetti
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp red chili flakes (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • Grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. cook the spaghetti
  2. heat the olive oil
  3. saute the garlic
  4. Add the chili flakes
  5. Toss in the spaghetti
  6. add a little reserved pasta water
  7. Stir in the chopped Parsley
  8. Serve immediately
